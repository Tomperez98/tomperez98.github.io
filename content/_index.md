I'm a software engineer from Medellín, Colombia, working at the intersection of **distributed systems** and **AI engineering**. I care about systems that behave predictably under concurrency and partial failure — and bringing AI agents, LLMs, and multimodal pipelines into production without sacrificing correctness or reliability.

My work lives at the boundary of research and practice: durable execution, formal methods for distributed protocols, serverless AI agent orchestration, and correctness-oriented system design. I build open-source tools like [**serverless-pydantic-ai**](https://github.com/Tomperez98/serverless-pydantic-ai) (durable AI agents on AWS Lambda) and [**formalizr**](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tomperez98/formalizr) (property-based fuzzing for stateful specs).

I write about **systems architecture**, **AI agent design**, **formal methods**, and whatever I'm tinkering with. This is where I share what I learn along the way.
